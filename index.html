◆ Enterprise E-Commerce Technology Transformation

Project Role: Lead Product Manager & Program Manager - Jewellery Division Duration: 8 months (2024-2025) Brands: Tanishq, Mia by Tanishq, Zoya Team: 40+ software engineers & professionals from Tata Consultancy Services

Titan's jewellery e-commerce ran on HCL WebSphere Commerce - outdated, costly to maintain, and disconnected from modern marketing, service, and fulfilment tools. Leadership mandated a replatform to Salesforce Commerce Cloud across three brands simultaneously. The hard part wasn't the platform choice - every connected system had to migrate in lockstep without breaking the live business.
